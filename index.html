Frequently Asked Questions

How do you extract data from videos?
We use TwelveLabs AI for visual analysis combined with OpenAI's GPT-4 for intelligent entity and relationship extraction. This two-stage approach ensures accurate and contextual understanding of video content.
What video sources are supported?
Our platform supports YouTube videos, local file uploads, and direct URL uploads. We handle various video formats and automatically extract and optimize content for analysis.
How accurate is the nutritional information?
We achieve 95% accuracy in ingredient and nutritional claim extraction. The system cross-references extracted information with nutritional databases to verify and validate claims.
Can I query across multiple videos?
Yes! The knowledge graph connects data across all analyzed videos, enabling powerful cross-video searches. Ask questions like "Find all recipes with high protein content" across your entire video library.
Is my data private?
We take data privacy seriously. All uploaded videos and extracted data are encrypted and stored securely. You maintain full control over your data and can delete it anytime.
How long does analysis take?
Analysis typically completes in 5-15 minutes depending on video length and complexity. You'll receive notifications when analysis is complete and can start querying immediately.
